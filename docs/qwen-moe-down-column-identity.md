# Qwen's repeated expert-down columns are dead producer coordinates

The [installed layer-0 rank witness](../../kelana/research/moe/installed-down-rank/README.md) found 2,006 distinct columns in one eight-expert route. A complete installed-image census and a join to **all forty paired gate/up masks** settle what that apparent output collapse buys: **nothing beyond already-counted producer dormancy**. Every coordinate belonging to a repeated decoded down-column class is a paired decoded Q4_K *all-zero gate/up row*. Thus its SwiGLU hidden value vanishes on every finite input in the real decoded-weight map. This is stronger than a short-text observation and applies to the whole installed image. Distinct-column rank on unrestricted independent hiddens is not the relevant reachable-domain rank at these coordinates.

Of 5,242,880 decoded expert-down columns across forty layers, **53,282 (1.0163%)** repeat an earlier column *within their own expert*. Including the representatives, **58,198 repeated-class coordinates** are paired gate/up dormant: zero counterexamples in all 10,240 matrices. Layer 0 contributes 50,239 of the 53,282 redundant columns (94.29%); layer 1 contributes 1,287, layer 2 669, and fifteen layers have none. All repeated down columns themselves are **nonzero FP32 vectors**. Three down layers use Q6_K, the other 37 Q5_K. The earlier held route's eight per-expert distinct counts (244, 210, 265, 264, 231, 252, 362, 178) reproduce exactly.

Disjoint actual 64-token train/held forty-layer producer captures independently confirm **0 nonzero post-SwiGLU values** among **103,198 / 107,796 selected repeated-class coordinates**, respectively. The gate/up zero-mask inclusion is the structural explanation, not an inference from those finite captures. The previously published [all-layer dormancy ceiling](qwen-moe-all-layer-dormancy.md) already charges free three-bank deletion of every paired dormant coordinate, including these. There is no additional independent column-identity saving to add to its **0.24754% held conditional complete one-read byte** figure.

If one incorrectly treats the 53,282 redundant columns as live independent hidden coordinates, free removal of duplicates after arbitrary repacking would remove **2,066,572 bytes/token train / 2,164,470 held**, just **0.07869% / 0.08242%** of the 2,626,187,904-byte complete one-read comparator. These are nominal Q5_K/Q6_K column-size comparisons, *not additive* to dormancy and not a native result. The selected packed blocks share scales over 256-input superblocks; deleting columns alters layout and possibly quantization. Nor does a real-valued zero-row proof establish bit-identical native FP32 state, signed zero or logits. No complete-model language quality or native TPS was measured. **Decision:** do not build a common-output exact column-interning reader from this rank collapse. A promising output observer needs changed learned directions on the active coordinates, a paid forty-layer image and held complete-model quality; ordinary Q8/expert phase diagnosis remains independent.

## Evidence

[`down_column_identity.py`](../tools/qwen-moe/down_column_identity.py) decodes all 10,240 selected-GGUF down matrices with the installed GGML Q5_K/Q6_K functions, proposes equal 2048-word FP32 columns by hash and resolves every candidate by complete **bitwise** word comparison (including signed zero). Its [forty source/model/library/tensor/route/hidden-hashed shard records and aggregate](../../data/qwen-moe/down-column-identity/receipt.json) retain all 256 per-expert counts and 512-bit repeated-class masks per layer. The [join checker](../tools/qwen-moe/down_column_join.py) checks each of 5,242,880 coordinates against independently decoded current-library gate/up zero masks: the [join receipt](../../data/qwen-moe/down-column-identity/join-receipt.json) records all forty equalities, library hashes and the 58,198-subset result. It also compares all 10,240 current-library masks with the prior [dormancy receipt](../../data/qwen-moe/all-layer-dormancy/aggregate.json); they agree bitwise despite the library revision change. [The data directory README](../../data/qwen-moe/down-column-identity/README.md) gives the CPU shard commands. The selected runtime, GPU lock and resident service were untouched.
