## Actual weight-stream budget

`tools/qwen-moe/traffic.py MODEL --output ../../data/qwen-moe/traffic.json`
reads GGUF metadata and the runtime's quantization block definitions. It counts
22,123,538,944 tensor payload bytes, of which 19,568,525,312 belong to routed
expert banks. Eight of 256 experts read 611,516,416 bytes per token. The
nonexpert weights cost 2,014,669,312 bytes, including a 417,177,600-byte Q6_K
output head and many Q8_0 attention projections. The embedding lookup adds one
2,176-byte row, not its whole 540 MB image.

Under one uncached read of each selected weight, that is 2,626,187,904 bytes
per token. At the machine's measured 242 GB/s bandwidth it costs 10.85 ms,
or 92.15 tokens/s before state/KV traffic, arithmetic, routing and launches.
This is a conditional traffic budget, not a universal limit or an achieved rate.
The routed experts account for only 23.3% of that stream. A MoE-specific
optimization can matter more for prompt batches, but single-token engineering
must also target the nonexpert projections and output head. The [gfx1151 known-stream counter calibration](qwen-moe-gl2c-calibration.md) reports half of each hashed 16–256 MiB load with both raw and `_sum` GL2C metrics. The [profiler-selection repair](qwen-moe-gl2c-attribution.md) recovers every one of 267 missing quantized counter calls in the native panel's 395-dispatch hole by selecting only quantized matvecs; broad selection retains the hole even without HIP graphs and with one metric. Complete positive quantized readings still report about half an image and cannot establish physical bytes or rereads.

[A complete installed nonembedding Q8 zero census](qwen-moe-q8-zero-bounds.md) finds all 28,416 zero weight blocks inside 444 whole rows, with no zero blocks in 243 of 250 nonembedding Q8 tensors. Even granting free exact sparse execution, skipping every such row removes at most 0.03679% of the complete modeled one-read stream; a paid one-bit-per-row mask nets 0.03362%. This closes sparse Q8 row skipping as a meaningful answer to the decode gap, not all alternative Q8 consumers. [The complete same-K packed-block census](qwen-moe-q8-block-reuse.md) finds no repeated **nonzero** Q8_0 32-code dot vector across any output rows within the same tensor: its 25,903 same-K duplicates are all zero-coded. [The routed-sum fusion audit](qwen-moe-sum-fusion.md) finds the seven selected-output additions already execute as one native kernel per layer. Its forty launches total only .125 ms/token of warm traced device duration; even deleting every binary multiply and this sum removes at most .331 ms/token under the same trace. Reassociating captured FP32 terms changes bits on every observed token. Do not port another sum-only kernel on the premise of seven launch gaps. The next native candidate should consume expert weights and carry the ordered sum through the down projection, then measure the complete expert path.

- Read the selected experts without expanding the full expert bank. Price the

- Carry a common output coordinate through the routed expert sum. For
  `D_e = C B_e`, form `sum_e a_e B_e h_e` first and apply `C` once. This is

  Fit and assess against actual expert outputs and routes, not only individual

  Prompt batches and independent decode streams have different expert occupancy.
  Include token gathering, reduction order and output scattering in the price.
- Co-design expert codes and their weighted output consumer. Charge dictionaries,
  scales, route metadata and activation-dependent work. Bonsai's ternary format
