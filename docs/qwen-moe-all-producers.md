# Forty-layer actual routed producer and output census

The layer-0 omission study could not establish whether later experts are easier to omit. A single callback capture now records the selected Qwen3.6-35B-A3B GGUF's **post-attention normalized 2,048-wide producer, eight routed IDs, normalized scores and eight 2,048-wide down outputs at every one of forty layers**. Two disjoint real-text inputs (the existing 113/126-token route-capture texts) were each cut to their first 64 tokens before decode. Every layer emitted all four tensors once. The first 64 layer-0 rows of **all four tensors are byte-for-byte identical** to the independently retained full-text layer-0 capture on both inputs, including all 1,048,576 down floats per split. This is actual quantized-runtime producer/output data, not synthetic activations or original BF16 routes. The callback itself cuts the graph, so its whole-model arithmetic is not the unobserved plain decode map.

For each layer and token, form the FP64 offline reference `sum_{e=0}^7 score[e] * down[e]`. Omit the lowest-scored expert without renormalizing; compare the omitted vector with this reference. A deliberately generous oracle may instead choose the **smallest** omitted-vector norm among all eight, with hindsight and no selection cost. The resulting ratios are *local* Euclidean errors of the routed sum, not model language loss, bit-identical native reductions or an inference-speed panel.

| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Train | .07597–.18133; **.14344** | .21676 | .37010 | .12480 | **0 / 104 of 2,560** |
| Held | .07115–.18690; **.14163** | .21605 | .36481 | .11857 | **0 / 115 of 2,560** |

The free per-token best-one oracle has minimum held error **.01053**; even with all eight true output vectors in hand, **not one** of 2,560 held layer-token decisions permits a one-expert omission below 1% of that token's routed-output norm. Lowest-score omission reaches below 5% on just 88/2,560 held layer-tokens. Layer 0's held one-skip RMS is .08186 on this 64-token subset, *less* than the forty-layer median: using its previous apparent ease as a general omission prior is optimistic. Observed held distinct expert IDs span 65–158 per layer, not a narrow fixed bank.

One omitted expert at every layer/token could ideally save one eighth of the 611,516,416-byte conditional selected expert stream, or **76,439,552 bytes / 2.9107%** of the 2,626,187,904-byte complete one-read weight comparator per token. But it incurs the measured local errors above. Even allowing the oracle to omit only on its 115 held decisions below 5% yields at most **.1308%** of that modeled complete weight stream when averaged across these layer-token observations, before selection, score/producer work, changed grouping, shared branch and any native time. This is a *finite observed family bound*: exact output omission at unchanged directions and scores, or hindsight selection of one of those same omitted vectors, not a theorem against trained replacements, another downstream observer, or a full-model quality tradeoff. At 5% local threshold a remaining expert could have cancellations on a different input; do not promote this local criterion to a language-quality budget.

The new information changes the representation question: do not extrapolate the layer-0 omission or tiny-score direction into all layers. The saved producer, IDs and score arrays can instead train **new input-dependent packed expert directions** across layers, freeze a paid complete image, and judge it by disjoint held language loss. The dense sub-bit and ternary pilots showed that locally better response fit may make the composed model worse. A native expert skip is not justified by this oracle; ordinary unprofiled Q8/expert phase measurement is independent.

## Reproduction and custody

`tools/qwen-moe/capture_all_producers.cpp` uses llama.cpp's `cb_eval` and the selected installed native libraries, no runtime source edit. It copies strided top-k and output tensors to contiguous token-major files, checks shapes/counts, and saves token IDs. `tools/qwen-moe/all_producer_geometry.py` independently checks dimensions, finite values, IDs and score order, then evaluates FP64 local errors. The [complete capture and receipt](../../data/qwen-moe/all-producers/README.md) retain every tensor file, source, selected executable and complete model hashes, text/token inputs, service-restoring wrapper logs and failed-work visibility. Both captures used `tools/run-batch-compare --runtime-max 39s --memory-gib 34 --host-reserve-gib 4 --exec`; the GPU lock is free and `bonsai-halo.service` is active. Host load was about 2.2 busy cores, so this callback panel establishes data and local response, **not timing**. No selected runtime, model, package or default changed.
