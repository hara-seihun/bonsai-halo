# Swapped Qwen batch loses the newest KV cell in one sequence

The [matched logical-sequence permutation panel](qwen-moe-gdn-permutation-equiv.md) feeds the same 32 sequences, tokens and positions to normal and row-0/1-swapped third-step batches. The [first-key diagnosis](qwen-moe-gdn-k-origin.md) identified a *different* row-4 temporal RoPE displacement. Here the observation is all saved FP16 K/V cells rather than one key or the head alone.

`tools/qwen-moe/gdn_zero_key.py` reads the four retained post-step serialized states and checks every one of **6,400** cells per arm (32 sequence slots × 10 attention layers × K/V × 10 positions). Each cell is 512 FP16 words / 1,024 bytes. Both normal arms contain **zero all-zero cells**. Both swapped arms contain exactly **20**: the newest (position-9) K **and** V cell in sequence slot 1, at **every one of the ten attention layers**. All 9 preceding K/V cells in this slot and all cells in the other 31 slots have at least one nonzero FP16 word. In fact, each of the 20 normal slot-1 newest cells has 512 nonzero words. The same pattern in the guarded direct and gathered arms rules out the direct GDN writer as the *sole* cause. The frozen third-step input associates physical batch row 0 with logical sequence 1 and row 1 with logical sequence 0; the saved destination is keyed by logical slot, not by the order of output heads.

[The full saved-cell relocation test](qwen-moe-gdn-row1-destination.md) additionally finds that none of the 20 missing normal K/V cells appears at any of the other 319 saved slot/position coordinates of its matching swapped layer/kind. All 32 streams' KV metadata remains byte-identical. A pure saved-cell permutation cannot repair this panel; producer, device write and export remain distinct possible boundaries.

This is a definite **saved-KV destination failure** on this swapped transition: a position-9 K/V cell for logical slot 1 is all positive zero where the normal transition saves nonzero data in every coordinate. It is not explained by merely applying the wrong RoPE angle to a nonzero projected key, because the value is also zero and RoPE does not transform V. It does **not** establish which forward graph operation wrote or failed to write that destination, whether the pre-attention K/V producer was zero, or whether a serialization/mapping step omitted live device data. The row-4 K-only temporal displacement is independent evidence; conflating the two would lead to a broad arithmetic rewrite for what may be two row-index/lifetime defects.

**Next native experiment:** in the *unchanged no-callback graph*, side-band capture the layer-3 pre-RoPE K and V, post-RoPE K, logical slot and write address for the batch row carrying sequence 1, alongside row 4's three IMRoPE position components. Capture after execution and before serialization as well as the serialized cell. If K/V producers are nonzero and the target slot is zero before serialization, inspect KV destination/commit mapping; if target device slot is nonzero and serialization produces zero, inspect state-export mapping. Continue to compare every full head and complete state. Do not repair a saved cell post hoc or weaken the selected guarded schedule.

[The hashed CPU receipt](../../data/qwen-moe/gdn-permutation-equiv/zero-key-receipt.json) includes every zero-cell coordinate, the three notable slots' nonzero counts, source/parser/probe and four entire state SHA-256s. Reproduce without GPU using `python3 tools/qwen-moe/gdn_zero_key.py --output ../../data/qwen-moe/gdn-permutation-equiv/zero-key-receipt.json`. No executable, model, service or numerical default changed; this is a finite state observation, not a throughput improvement.
