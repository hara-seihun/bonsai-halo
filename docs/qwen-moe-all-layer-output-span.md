# Actual Qwen routed-output finite-span boundary

[Kelana's forty-layer output-span study](../../kelana/research/moe/all-layer-output-span/README.md) uses actual selected Qwen3.6-35B-A3B routed down vectors and normalized scores on disjoint 64-token prompt captures. At every layer it grants a free optimal projection into **all 512 weighted down-vector directions observed in train**. The rank-512 span loses **.704919 pooled held relative RMS** of the complete eight-expert weighted sum; each layer loses at least .558547 (median .799481). The final layer contributes 34.28% of the pooled denominator; without it the held RMS is .770314. Rank-256 train-slot PCA loses .774955 pooled held RMS.

The [source/input/model-hashed CPU receipt](../../data/qwen-moe/all-layer-output-span/receipt.json) retains forty per-layer squared-error ratios and all 160 input-array hashes. These are ideal-real local orthogonal projection errors, not paid image rate, full-model loss, native FP32 identity or time. The earlier conditional equal-effective-byte rank-512 factoring could save no more than **6.55% of whole-model one-read weight bytes** even if extended to all layers; this measured short-capture quality rules out porting that coordinate. It does not reject a basis learned from broader producer text or from the entire installed expert bank. A new MoE image needs broad real routed training, a disjoint complete-model language-quality result at paid rate, and a direct-consumer cost before native adoption. Ordinary Q8/expert physical execution remains a separate engine question; no runtime, GPU or service state changed here.
