Neither float32 column is the exact real map, and neither reproduces the
other's bits. For ||k|| <= 1 and beta in (0,1) the per-token operator
g_t (I - beta_t k_t k_t^T) has norm at most 1, so an error injected at one
token is not amplified by later tokens; errors are still injected at every
token and accumulate. These are observations on these random operands, not
a bound, and they say nothing about the triangular system's condition
number, which is not established here for unit k either.
