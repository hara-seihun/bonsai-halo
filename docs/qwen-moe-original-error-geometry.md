# Original-versus-installed routed error does not cancel between gate/up and down

The [first-sixteen original-expert comparison](qwen-moe-original-expert-error.md) established a real-producer local error. This follow-up asks a narrower computation question: can the existing Q4_K gate/up and Q5_K down **errors cancel in the actual score-weighted routed sum**, making an independent projection objective misleading in this particular subset? It uses the same pinned official BF16 layer-0 tensors, installed GGUF, disjoint 64-token producer/ID/normalized-score captures and offline FP32 BLAS. Other routed experts remain absent from the error vector, not imputed as original.

Write `B` for the official matched scored sum; `G` for replacing gate/up only, `D` for replacing down only and `Q` for replacing both. In FP64 scored output coordinates set `g=G−B`, `d=D−B`, and `i=Q−G−D+B`. The identity `Q−B=g+d+i` is exact for the computed outputs; it does **not** assert identical native FP32 accumulation. The observed squared error of `g+d` is `||g||²+||d||²+2⟨g,d⟩`, so the measured cosine and cross term directly price cancellation, without fitting a scalar or inventing a new image.

| Split | Matched assignments / tokens | `||g||/||B||` | `||d||/||B||` | `cos(g,d)` | `||g+d||/||B||` | `||i||/||B||` | `||Q−B||/||B||` |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Train | 25 / 24 | .04978 | .02774 | +.0021955 | .057041 | .001541 | .056955 |
| Held | 26 / 25 | .03577 | .02811 | −.0005786 | .045487 | .001124 | .045315 |

On held producers, the cross term `2⟨g,d⟩/(||g||²+||d||²)` is **−.0005622**: gate/up and down errors barely cancel in this scored output. The nonlinear interaction `i` changes the final relative norm by only .000172. Treating this quantized subset's down error as if gate/up error will cancel it is not supported; both banks need independent improvement or a genuinely learned joint direction. This is a local finite-observation negative, not a reason to reject jointly trained quantization. Quantized gate/up changes the down input, and whole-model loss may weight output directions very differently.

Within a token, matched experts almost never overlap: only **one train and one held token** have two or more of the first sixteen selected experts. The ratio of complete scored-error energy to the sum of individual scored-error energies is .99805 train / 1.00031 held, but this is primarily *coverage*, **not** proof that errors of eight simultaneous experts are orthogonal. Broad official tensors and route-diverse producers across forty layers are required to answer that question. These 51 assignments do not establish language NLL, a paid new image, native FP32 logit identity, or TPS. The selected runtime, model and service have not changed.

[`original_error_geometry.py`](../tools/qwen-moe/original_error_geometry.py) independently decodes the three installed first-sixteen tensor banks and checks original tensor, capture, inventory, model and decoder provenance. `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 python3 tools/qwen-moe/original_error_geometry.py` writes the [complete source/input/payload-hashed receipt and train/held energies](../../data/qwen-moe/original-expert-error/geometry.json) to the original-expert data owner. This is CPU-only. The next useful MoE representation experiment is a *paid*, jointly learned gate/up/down image on broad original routed producers selected by disjoint complete-model quality and priced for a packed native consumer, rather than another scalar fit to this sparse subset.
