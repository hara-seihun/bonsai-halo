# The counter-free Qwen trace's 64-ms idle interval is not a native decode phase

The [depth-1024 selected-runtime phase panel](qwen-moe-q8-unprofiled.md) reports 20.398 ms/token summed device kernel durations but roughly 88 ms between vocabulary heads in its `rocprofv3 --kernel-trace` run. Could the difference be a host-launch bottleneck that should outrank Q8 and routed experts? The trace itself gives a decisive negative for treating that **profiled** interval as native host scheduling time.

[`launch_gap.py`](../tools/qwen-moe/launch_gap.py) partitions each of the eight 1,565-dispatch decode tokens after the two synthetic-depth setup evaluations. It sorts by device start timestamp, sums positive end-to-next-start gaps, and verifies that kernel durations plus gaps exactly span every token's first-start to last-end interval (no overlapping dispatches in these windows). Seven warm tokens have median **20.694 ms kernel busy, 64.055 ms idle, 84.644 ms total**. Eight intervals ≥0.5 ms account for **58.623 ms**, or **91.52% of the median idle**. Their ordinal positions after the token begins are precisely **30, 62, 126, 254, 510, 766, 1022, 1278**: `2^5−2`, `2^6−2`, `2^7−2`, then every 256 dispatches. All seven warm tokens reproduce the first seven positions; six reproduce the last, while one anomalous token has three other 6–30-ms pauses. The first four delays approximately double with the powers-of-two positions; the later four are near 9–11 ms each. They land across unrelated kernel pairs (expert Q4, quantizer, top-k, row gather, copy), **not** repeatedly at a Q8 or expert layer boundary. This exact ordinal progression is characteristic of trace/instrumentation batching, though the GPU timestamps alone do not identify the profiler's internal cause.

Even deleting all eight regular long gaps leaves ~5.4 ms of small profiled gaps across 1,564 dispatch boundaries; it does not prove those small gaps disappear in normal execution. The separate unprofiled 32-token wall panel in the owning report measured **50.929 tokens/s**, or **19.635 ms/token**, with recorded host contention. That observation rules out using the profiled 84.6-ms token interval as native throughput or an addressable 64-ms launch opportunity. The profiled busy time itself exceeds the unprofiled wall time and cannot be subtracted from it to estimate native host overhead; instrumentation changes the run. The 20.4-ms family duration sum remains useful as a within-trace *ranking*, not a calibrated wall-time decomposition.

**Decision:** Do not optimize a fabricated 64-ms/token host gap, and do not interpret the trace's recurring 256-dispatch stalls as the native Q8/expert slow regime. The next informative engine experiment is same-binary, unprofiled per-layer device markers and a paired whole-model wall panel with equal heads/context and GPU-wrapper service restoration; compare ordinary and slow matvec calls inside the same instrument, not traced timestamp gaps against unprofiled wall. No runtime, GPU model, service or output map changed in this CPU analysis.

The [raw derived receipt](../../data/qwen-moe/q8-unprofiled/launch-gap.json) contains every interval ≥0.5 ms on each of eight tokens, complete token sums, the trace/source hashes and per-ordinal warm statistics. The owning [original receipt](../../data/qwen-moe/q8-unprofiled/receipt.json) binds this trace to pinned source, model, executable, wall samples, clocks and service state. Reproduce without a GPU:

```sh
python3 tools/qwen-moe/launch_gap.py \
  ../../data/qwen-moe/q8-unprofiled/trace/gpu-host/1886571_kernel_trace.csv \
  --out ../../data/qwen-moe/q8-unprofiled/launch-gap.json
```
