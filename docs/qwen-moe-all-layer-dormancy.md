# The layer-0 dormant-bank effect does not generalize across Qwen's forty layers

The [layer-0 gate/up/down finding](qwen-moe-zero-mask-mechanism.md) suggested as much as **9.212%** of complete-model one-read weight traffic could disappear if its 39.562% routed dormant-coordinate rate recurred in every layer and a joint sparse image were free. A complete installed-GGUF census refutes that premise: **59,096 / 5,242,880 (1.127%)** expert-channel positions have *both* Q4_K gate and up rows decoding to all zero across forty layers. Layer 0 contains **54,257 of the 59,096 (91.812%)**. Layers 1–4 fall to 2,153, 952, 370 and 181 respectively; six of the forty layers have no paired zero row. On the disjoint held 126-row actual prompt routes, **220,662 / 20,643,840 (1.069%)** selected slot-coordinates are paired dormant. Layer 0 supplies 204,177 of these, 92.529%. Train has 188,521 / 18,513,920 (1.018%), 93.496% from layer 0.

The gate and up row masks match at all but **one** of 5,242,880 positions: layer 13 expert 71 channel 339 has a zero up row but nonzero gate row. The conservative *paired* census excludes it. A zero in either input can make the real-number SwiGLU coordinate vanish; an independently measured FP32/image proposal could recover this one extra position. The newly computed layer-0 masks reproduce all 256 masks in the preceding two-half layer-0 receipt, a direct cross-check on indexing and the decoder.

## Paid-coordinate and traffic contract

An installed Q4_K gate or up row is 2,048 weights at 4.5 bits each, or **1,152 bytes**. A paired dormant coordinate saves 2,304 bytes from the two banks if the row can be omitted without changing its retained neighbors' quantization. Across all 10,240 expert tuples this is **136,157,184 raw bytes** of gate/up rows; explicit uncompressed 512-bit masks cost **655,360 bytes** (net at most 135,501,824 bytes before offsets/indexing and alignment). The selected GGUF has no such sparse image or consumer.

At the held prompt's *observed selected IDs*, the free paired gate/up row deletion would save **4,034,962 bytes/token**, or **0.15364%** of the established 2,626,187,904-byte/token complete-model one-read comparator. Train gives 3,843,826 bytes/token, **0.14637%**. Even granting a *further* free proportional deletion of every corresponding Q5_K down input coordinate (1,408 bytes per coordinate), with no re-quantization or packing cost, the held all-three-bank ceiling is **0.24754%** of those one-read bytes; train is **0.23581%**. That last bound grants arbitrary coordinate remapping across Q5_K groups and is not an exact unchanged-image layout. It is a data-dependent conditional weight-stream ceiling for these prompt routes, **not** a bound on compute or device time, generated tokens, or all possible inputs.

For the decoded real-weight map, deleting a channel whose gate and up rows are zero preserves the expert's output for every finite producer input: both dots vanish, SwiGLU vanishes and its down term vanishes. That does **not** establish the same FP32 bits if the native Q8_1 preparation, group scales, accumulation order, signed zeros or packed image change. In particular, the down Q5_K rows remain dense in the installed image. No serving map, installed runtime, model image, GPU reservation or resident service changed in this experiment.

**Decision.** Do not build the proposed all-layer joint sparse expert reader from the layer-0 percentage. The actual full-model conditional byte prize is over thirty-seven times smaller than the extrapolated 9.212% even after granting free down compaction (9.212 / 0.24754). A layer-0-specific exact skip might still reduce its *local* compute, but its complete-model byte opportunity is too small to justify a forty-layer sparse image first. The next independent MoE question is the steady ordinary Q8 / Q4-Q5 native cost with unprofiled phase markers, or the paid small-group MMQ dispatch already constructed on real routes. Those can move an actual complete-model phase without assuming dormant channels in 39 layers where they barely occur.

## Evidence

[The CPU source](../tools/qwen-moe/all_layer_dormancy.py) calls the selected `libggml-base.so` Q4_K dequantizer on both complete gate/up banks per layer, checks each decoded 512×2048 expert for all-zero rows, and weights the common mask against the forty native-selected-ID prompt captures. It records source, decoder, model and traffic-inventory identities, each route-file hash, all 10,240 packed 512-bit masks and a digest of each shard. The [data owner](../../data/qwen-moe/all-layer-dormancy/README.md) keeps all forty shards and the aggregate receipt, SHA-256 `f11a91ea2aac275c59d5a99a74354e46dfbf27b03c672509aabe88b3a2257624`. Native arithmetic, down-bank decoding beyond the separately checked layer 0, and complete-model quality were not changed or measured.
