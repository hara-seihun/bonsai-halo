# Expert-index gate/up sharing bound

[Kelana's finite-image study](../../kelana/research/moe/expert-axis/README.md) tests whether Qwen3.6's gate/up experts can reuse a few **full-width** matrix-vector responses before their separate SwiGLU and down paths. It is independent of the shared input-coordinate and shared down-output-coordinate experiments. All sixteen official layer-0 BF16 gate/up matrices have maximum absolute pairwise flattened cosine .01362. The best rank-seven expert-index basis retains 56.50% of their isotropic squared preactivation response. It consumes 87.84% of eight unfactored expert gate/up logical products per token, before routing and mixing. Rank fourteen first reaches 90% energy and costs 175.68% of those products. In a 32-input Gaussian, eight-of-sixteen synthetic route panel, the rank-seven shared map incurs .616 relative RMS at the router-weighted post-SwiGLU/down sum.

The bound is exact for a linear rank-limited factor of the sixteen flattened original gate/up matrices under isotropic inputs. The route panel is a composed *conditional diagnostic*, not an end-to-end quality or latency comparison with the mixed-Q4 llama.cpp reader. No executable or service changed. [Raw source/image-hashed receipt](../../data/qwen-moe/expert-axis/README.md) retains the panel. The next construction should use real routed activations and a shared cheap input preparation with expert-specific low-rate residuals, without forcing the whole original matrix bank into an expert-index span.
