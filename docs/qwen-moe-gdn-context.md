# Full-model guarded GDN at a real 512-token occupied prefix

The selected pinned Qwen3.6-35B-A3B runtime `7861dc7` retains its exact in-place generated-state advantage beyond the previously measured eight-token seed. A new probe seeds **32 independent sequences with 512 actual tokens each**, in 64 eight-token batches per sequence, saves the complete context and the 32 next IDs once, then loads the **same serialized context and IDs** in the gathered (`GGML_CUDA_GDN_FUSED_ROWS=0`) and installed fused arms. Each of five generated calls requests **all 32 × 248,320 vocabulary logits**, feeds back each stream's greedy choice and times the complete `llama_decode` plus synchronization. Both arms start from the same 2.3-GB saved state. This is occupied context, not a reserved KV capacity or an input-dependent step moved outside the timed generation interval; prefill is intentionally excluded from *both* generation rates.

## Why a saved common producer was necessary

A naive 128/512-token control/default panel appeared to change nearly every generated logit. Its *seed itself* was not reproducible: at depth 128, two identical gathered controls produced different seed heads, but a gathered/fused pair whose seed heads matched also matched every generated logit bit. At depth 512, two gathered seed runs agreed while two fused seed runs differed from **each other**; all 32 seed heads changed on the compared pairs, including two top-1 choices. That does not isolate a fused-GDN error. In the raw five-step panels (without seed capture), time moved in the same favorable direction, but their differing logits are not a valid exact-map speed comparison. A first depth-512 attempt with only 8192 total context slots failed at a 256-row batch (`failed to find a memory slot` and ROCm page fault); the probe now reserves enough slots for all 32 truly occupied prefixes and generated rows. The wrapper restored the resident service after that failed process. Do not compare different prompts or random initial state to certify an exact optimization.

The saved-context replay removes this confound and is the decisive comparison. It does **not** explain the nondeterministic multi-chunk seed. Its root cause—prompt graph/allocator state, router or recurrent producer—is a separate numerical reproducibility question. Direct side-band seed/layer capture is preferable to inferring an in-place GDN failure from mismatched initial states. The runtime's strict identity-map guard and gathered fallback for all other shapes remain correct as installed; the row-alias question on reordered IDs is owned separately.

Both paired samples are full-model wall times, not isolated GDN kernel time. After the last call the service was active and GPU lock free.
