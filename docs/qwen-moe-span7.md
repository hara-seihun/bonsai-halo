# Seven-output reuse on actual Qwen routes

[Kelana's seven-output study](../../kelana/research/moe/span7/README.md) asks whether seven already-computed score-weighted expert outputs can linearly reconstruct the eighth at layer 0 of the installed Qwen3.6-35B-A3B GGUF. The FP64 oracle knows the missing output and fits seven coefficients separately for each token and choice of missing expert. On 113 train and 126 held captured tokens, *no choice of seven* reaches 1% local relative Euclidean error on any token. Its best-choice held RMS is .06257. The score-prefix seven reaches .08228; a fixed train-fitted seven-gain correction scores .08334 held. No arithmetic or native inference gain follows from fitting these coefficients after the missing output is known.

A more generous candidate adds output directions learned from the train score-prefix residuals. At 32 directions and with free hindsight coefficients for all seven outputs and all 32 directions, held RMS moves only .08228 to .08096 and zero held tokens reach 1%. Train RMS falls to .04762. This strongly separates a fit to 113 producer tokens from a replacement direction that generalizes. At 5% local error, the any-seven oracle qualifies 53/126 held tokens; if all forty layers matched and choosing coefficients cost nothing, that is at most 1.224% of conditional whole-model one-read bytes with one expert omitted on eligible tokens. It is not the earlier [multi-omission subset oracle](../../kelana/research/moe/subset-oracle/README.md)'s 1.686% ceiling.

The [source/input-hashed CPU receipt](../../data/qwen-moe/span7/receipt.json) and Kelana script specify the FP64 observation, producer capture and free-oracle assumptions. This bound covers linear reuse of retained vectors plus the particular train-SVD global correction, not route-specific nonlinear replacements, other layers or complete-model language quality. No GPU run, installed target, selected weights or Bonsai service changed. The next representation experiment needs substantially larger disjoint real routes and route-conditioned missing directions, then paid-image complete-model loss. Exact-engine work should meanwhile resolve the unprofiled Q8 and routed Q4/Q5 phase gap rather than port this oracle.
