# First row-4 gathered key difference is a temporal-position rotation

The [matched 31+1 gathered permutation panel](qwen-moe-gather-permutation-layer.md) first changes logical row 4's *saved* layer-3 key at the third decode step; its preceding nine K cells, first V cell and recurrent R/S layers 0–2 remain exact. Both executions pass the same logical sequence/token at position **9**. This CPU analysis of their retained FP16 key cells identifies the changed coordinate much more narrowly than a general K-producer mismatch.

Qwen3.6's pinned configuration specifies two 256-dimensional key heads, 64 rotary dimensions per head, `mrope_interleaved`, sectors `[11,11,10]` and base `1e7`. The selected native IMRoPE kernel couples channels `i` and `i+32` in each head; temporal-sector pairs are `i=0,3,...,30`. **All 31 changed FP16 words lie in these 44 temporal-sector halfwords; the other 468 words are bit-identical.** Of those 31, 15 are in the first head and 16 in the second. The first V image remains bit-identical. The swapped execution also saves **512 zero FP16 words** for the entire row-1 layer-3 K cell, a distinct fault worth following rather than attributing to row 4's temporal rotation.

For each of the 22 temporal pairs, compute the relative angle of the two saved FP16 vectors, then compare it with `Δp × 1e7^(-i/32)` for integer `Δp ∈ [-32,32]`, with angular error weighted by original pair norm. The unique best integer displacement is **−9 positions**: weighted angular RMS **0.00007187 radians**, max error **0.0003537**; the next-best displacement (−10) is **0.08613 radians** RMS, roughly 1,198 times worse. Re-rotating the *already rounded FP16* control key by −9 and rounding again reproduces **500/512 saved words** (32/44 temporal words), with at most **0.001953125** absolute error in the remaining twelve; double rounding prevents demanding exactness here. Pair lengths differ by at most .001574. The same −9 angle fits both heads; it is precisely the difference between the recorded logical position 9 and zero.

This is a finite **post-RoPE observation**, not a proof that the pre-RoPE K projection and normalization were bit-identical or that the position *buffer* necessarily held zero. But it is strong evidence for a misplaced temporal position/address or a RoPE output lifetime at the row-4 attention boundary, rather than propagated layer-0–2 recurrent damage in row 4. The next native no-callback probe should capture row 4's three `inp_pos` components at the layer-3 IMRoPE read and its normalized pre-RoPE K, then the K output and write destination; separately trace why swapped row 1 writes a zero K cell. Preserve full-head and serialized-state comparison. Do **not** patch saved K after the fact or generalize this one input to sequence-order invariance.
